# Data requirements

The **dataQCtools** package was initially developed for the Hoh Tribe
water quality data and is therefore structured to handle data in their
format. This page describes the data, how it is collected, and what
their workflow is to preprocess, analyze, and report this data. If you
are a Tribal environmental professional and your workflow differs from
what is described below, please feel free to reach out to us and we can
discuss how to adapt **dataQCtools** for your use case.
